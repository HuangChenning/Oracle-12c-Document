`DBA_ARGUMENTS` lists the arguments of the functions and procedures that are available in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ARGUMENTS`.

See Also:

- " [ALL\_ARGUMENTS](ALL_ARGUMENTS.md) "
- " [DBA\_PROCEDURES](DBA_PROCEDURES.md) " for information about the functions and procedures that are available in the database
